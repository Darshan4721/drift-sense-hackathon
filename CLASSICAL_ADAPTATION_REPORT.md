# Classical Stage Adaptation & Prefilter Experiment Report

> **Dataset Scope Disclosure:** All results below were measured strictly **on our synthetic benchmark sets** generated by `drift_sense.synthetic`. Performance is **never claimed on real fab images**.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Verdict

We evaluated classical stage adaptation for degraded SEM images by adding optional prefiltering (`--prefilter`: `none`, `dog`, `log`, `lcn`) and candidate pool enlargement (`--candidate-pool`: `16`, `32`) to [`localize.py`](file:///D:/tmp/erode_hackathon/localize.py) and [`evaluate.py`](file:///D:/tmp/erode_hackathon/evaluate.py). Default behavior remains strictly `none` (OFF) and `16` candidates.

### **Definitive Conclusion:**
- **Negative Result:** Prefiltering (Difference-of-Gaussians, Laplacian-of-Gaussian, and Local Contrast Normalization) **severely harms** localization performance on semiconductor inspection images, causing a **14 to 18 percentage point drop in accuracy** on degraded sets and increasing error on default sets.
- **Enlarging candidate pool to 32** yielded **0.00% accuracy improvement** (exactly 87/150 = 58.00%).
- **Recommendation:** As dictated by empirical evidence, **leave the default prefilter OFF (`none`)**.

## 4. Why Do Prefilters Fail on Semiconductor SEM Images?

1. **Suppression of Macro-Spatial Context:**
   In periodic structures (like DRAM capacitor arrays), individual cells are visually identical. What disambiguates the true block from adjacent periodic cells in cross-correlation is the low-to-mid frequency global contrast envelope (boundary terminations, peripheral circuitry, density gradients). High-pass / band-pass filters (DoG, LoG, LCN) subtract local means and attenuate low frequencies, reducing the image to pure local edges. This transforms the cross-correlation surface into hundreds of identical-height peaks, drastically worsening periodic aliasing.
2. **Noise Amplification:**
   Second-derivative filters like Laplacian-of-Gaussian amplify high-frequency speckle noise on severely degraded images, degrading SNR and confusing the classical peak finder.
3. **Verifier Disconnection:**
   Enlarging the candidate pool from 16 to 32 did not improve accuracy because the verifier guardrail (`learned_best.classical_score + 0.02 < classical_best.classical_score`) properly rejects learned candidate shifts when verifier confidence on degraded out-of-distribution patches is low.
